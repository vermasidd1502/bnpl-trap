ℹ️ How to read this (CAPM, beta, VaR, Monte Carlo)

Beta = how hard the market (Nifty) moves you. β=1.2 → you swing ~1.2× Nifty. Systematic risk (market/beta) can't be diversified away; idiosyncratic (stock-specific) can — by holding uncorrelated names.

VaR (Value at Risk) = the loss you'd exceed only X% of the time (99% = a 1-in-100 day). ES (Expected Shortfall) = the *average* loss when you breach VaR — the fat-tail number. Student-t VaR > Normal VaR because real markets have fat tails (FIN-537).
