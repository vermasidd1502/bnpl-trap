accumulation ✕ Friday strength ✕ room-to-run — which stocks to long over the weekend
ℹ️ The theory — accumulation/distribution & the weekend effect

Weekend / Monday effect: historically Mondays printed the lowest (often negative) returns, Fridays the highest (French 1980, Gibbons–Hess) — driven by bad news after the Friday close, settlement timing, and dealers flattening into the weekend. It has faded in modern markets, so the raw effect is a weak edge — *unconditional* weekend-holds lose.

Accumulation vs distribution is the institutional footprint. Accumulation = up-closes in the upper range on rising volume (Chaikin A/D & OBV rising, few distribution days). Distribution = weak/down closes on higher volume (O'Neil "distribution days"; ≥5 in 25 sessions = institutions selling). Buying Friday strength only pays when the name is being accumulated, not distributed.

India: day-of-week effects are mixed and weakening; weekly expiry adds noise. The anchor is our own backtest: strong-Friday momentum → +0.70% Monday, while extended/at-ceiling names show no edge. So the favorable weekend long = accumulation + Friday strength + room-to-run + a positive weekend-edge history.
