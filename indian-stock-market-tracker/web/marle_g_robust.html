What each test means
Net Sharpe
After realistic Indian round-trip costs (delivery STT + slippage). Turnover-aware: daily-flatten strategies pay every day; a positional swing pays only when the basket changes.
PSR — Probabilistic Sharpe
P(true Sharpe > 0), adjusting for skew & fat tails. Want > 0.95. Low PSR = the Sharpe could easily be noise.
DSR — Deflated Sharpe
PSR after correcting for how many strategies were searched. Picking the best of ~16 inflates Sharpe; DSR is the selection-honest probability. Want > 0.90.
Bootstrap CI & subperiods
95% confidence band on the Sharpe from block-resampling, plus the Sharpe in 4 time slices. If 0 is inside the band or slices flip sign, the edge isn't stable.
