METHOD & PROVENANCE
Data sources: SPY / VIX / Treasury futures / sector ETFs / commodity futures via yfinance; macro econ series via FRED. Cache: 5-minute server-side TTL on every endpoint, with FRED at 60-minute (data updates monthly/weekly, not intraday). Regime classifier: SPY 20d realised vol vs 60d, SPY vs SMA-50, VIX threshold at 25 — published in Apollo Hermes v7.0::market_regime. This is real-money-grade data but research-grade plumbing — no failover provider, no SLA. For production use, wire to Bloomberg MIL or a paid Polygon feed.
