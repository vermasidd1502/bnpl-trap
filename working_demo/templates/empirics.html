08 Equity Calendar-Time α — The Honest Null t = 0.08 paper §6.9 · the empirical motivation for everything that follows
cohort-aggregated alpha
t = 0.08
CAPM / Fama-French 3-factor · 2018–2024
rule-of-thumb threshold |t| > 2

Rp,t = α + β₁·MKTt + β₂·SMBt + β₃·HMLt + εt

Rp = monthly portfolio return of all BSI-driven shorts
α = excess return left over after market / size / value risk
t(α) = does that excess differ from random noise?

Reconciliation: the signal still works (Granger 23/27, panel β = −0.037 p=0.04), but the equity wrapper averages CVNA +96% / UPST +88% wins against losses on growth-stage false positives like SEZL (−600% if shorted). The average is statistical zero. This is an instrument-selection problem, not a signal-quality problem (paper §7.2 diagnosis).

12 Honest Disclosures paper §8 limitations · in one place
ItemStatus
Sample size n = 6 firms / 15 sub-eventsSmall relative to empirical-finance journal standards. Wilson 95% CI [56.6%, 100%] still carries meaningful uncertainty. Path to n ≥ 50: extend backward to 2011 OR add adjacent product classes.
CURO conditional on event-date ruleDetected at 720d under bankruptcy-filing convention; near-miss under tighter rule. Headline 5/5 invariant to ±90d shifts but conditional on event-date RULE choice.
EWMA (exponentially-weighted moving average) z-scoring vs raw log-CFPB volume in panelRaw log-volume marginally outperforms EWMA composite by AIC in panel-regression specification. BSI's value is in cross-firm threshold comparability for event detection, not in panel regression. Documented honestly.
Equity calendar-time α is statistically zerot = 0.08. Documented as a finding rather than concealed. Empirical motivation for the §9.1 fixed-income deployment.
Long-pod doesn't beat naive momentum on same firmsMann-Whitney p > 0.4 at every horizon vs same-firm random-date baseline. Architecture is a wave-riding conviction filter, not standalone alpha.
Absence of TRACE / firm-level CDS dataArchitectural specification of §9.1 fixed-income deployment is presented; backtested-alpha demonstration left to follow-up paper conditional on TRACE / Markit access.
Coverage gaps in auxiliary pillarsFirm-vitality pillar (LinkedIn / X) and Google Trends pillar are intermittently rate-limited. Composite computes correctly via coverage-gate γi; CFPB and MOVE load-bearing pillars are fresh-through.
Survivorship bias in long-pod cohortCURO and TRICOLOR can't appear because their equity wiped out before the recovery test could fire. Tested-cohort hit rate is conditional on survival.
