Two-system architecture

Behavioural data on one side. Risk discipline on the other.

BearWatch reads the consumer-side signal — eight pillars of complaint, sentiment, and macro data — and converts it to a daily firm-level z-score. Apollo Hermes turns each signal into a sized, gated, journaled trade. Same pipeline, two layers, full audit trail.

Sensing layer

BearWatch

  • 8-pillar Behavioural Stress Index
  • 11-state deterministic bear engine
  • 4-gate AND-rule (BSI · SCP · MOVE · CCD II)
  • 5-of-6 historical hit rate, 14-mo lead
  • Cannot execute. Cannot size. Cannot enforce risk.
Acting layer

Apollo Hermes

  • 7 pre-trade risk checks
  • Drawdown engine · cash floors · position caps
  • Live yfinance + Finnhub + Polymarket
  • Trade journal + Gemini post-mortem
  • Cannot generate alpha — needs upstream signal

★ Honest framing

Backtested simulation. No live capital deployed.

Every result on this site is a backtest applied retroactively to historical CFPB / Reddit / yfinance data. The methodology was built in 2026 and applied to 2018–2025 events. The 4-gate architecture has a documented partial-fail under macro shocks (Sprint 6.1) and a known data-ingest gap for marketplace-lender disclosures (Sprint 6.5a). Both fixes are queued. The deliverable's credibility comes from disclosing what doesn't work, not from claiming everything does.
