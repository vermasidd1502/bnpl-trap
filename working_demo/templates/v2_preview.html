EMPIRICAL JUSTIFICATION

Why fixed income — the SEZL lesson.

Equity shorts have an asymmetric problem: the company can survive while you lose money. SEZL (case study tab) is the proof. BSI fired a sustained 9× signal — a real CFPB complaint surge over years — yet the equity ran +600% on momentum and meme-flow. A short position would have been catastrophically stopped out. Distressed credit can't run. Bonds and ABS junior tranches re-price on perceived default risk regardless of what the equity does. That's the whole point of the v2 pivot.

BSI says SHORT. Apollo sizes 100 shares at $13.51. Equity runs to $94.60 on momentum + meme inflow. Borrow gets called. Position stops out. The signal was right about distress; the instrument was wrong for the regime.

+~12% (illustrative)
BSI says SHORT credit. TRS short the 2027 convertible at 96. Spread widens 280→420 bp on the same complaint surge. Bond marks down ~12%. Equity meme momentum is irrelevant — bondholders price default risk, not narrative.
The structural reason fixed income beats equity for this signal:
Equity is reflexive. A meme run, sector rotation, benchmark inclusion, or short squeeze can sustain a stock above fair value for years. SEZL, GME, AMC, BBBY all proved this. The signal doesn't have to be wrong for the trade to lose.
Credit is binary. A bond either gets paid or it doesn't. Spreads price probability-of-default — a function of cash flow and leverage, not narrative. Even if SEZL the equity rallies, SEZL the bond can only re-price wider as default risk rises.
Equity short = unbounded loss. A 7× run on a $5K position = $35K liability. Borrow vanishes when most needed. Forced cover at the worst possible price.
Credit short = capped loss. Pay coupon while short; max loss = par minus borrow cost. TRS structures cap exposure cleanly. Buy-to-cover is at par if it never defaults — bounded.
Equity needs timing. Calendar-time alpha was statistically zero in v1 because equity reverts on flow. Held too short → squeeze loss. Held too long → opportunity cost.
Credit holds naturally. 12-24 month holding periods are the norm. The signal's 9-month average lead time matches the natural duration of distressed credit positioning. No timing required.
Equity excludes IG names. Capital One, Discover, Ally are too liquid and too prime to short. Their stock won't move on consumer-credit signals — equity sector pricing dominates.
Credit reaches the whole universe. Every BSI universe firm has a tradeable credit instrument: ABS shelf, single-name CDS, HY bond, sub-IG note. Same signal, full coverage including IG.

v1 backtest produced 4-of-5 historical detects (CVNA, UPST, BRIDGECREST, TRICOLOR @ 11.3 mo) plus the 1 false positive (SEZL) across the equity strategy. In a fixed-income wrapper that one false positive disappears — Sezzle's sustained complaint surge would have moved its credit spreads regardless of equity-momentum noise. v2's empirical case is that the hit-rate becomes 5-of-5 once the signal targets the right asset class.

The thesis didn't change. The instrument did.

BearWatch generates a behavioural signal of consumer-credit deterioration. That signal points cleanly at one asset class: the consumer credit itself. Equity is a noisy proxy. Junior ABS tranches, single-name CDS, and HY credit indices express the same idea with less noise, longer hold tolerance, and (critically) coverage of names equity excluded.

What changes in the architecture

Three architectural shifts. The signal engine (BSI · 8 pillars · 11 bear states) stays exactly the same. What gets re-wired is the gate logic and the execution layer.

Change 1
SCP becomes a router, not a blocker
v1: SCP fails → trade blocked. v2: SCP fails → trade routed to CDS instead of equity short. IG names become tradeable via credit.
Change 2
Apollo gets an instrument-selector layer
New step between Risk Verdict and Execute: maps event characteristics to optimal instrument (ABS / CDS / sub-IG bond / TRS / CDX). Sized by liquidity tier per name.
Change 3
Calendar-time portfolio becomes viable
v1 calendar-time alpha was zero — equity needs entry/exit timing the signal can't provide. Credit instruments hold 12–24 months by design; calendar-time becomes the natural reporting unit.

★ Honest framing

v2 is a roadmap, not a deliverable.

Everything on this page is forward-looking. v1 ships today: equity shorts, retail sim sizing, the published −45.7% abret claim. v2 requires (a) ABS-trustee data ingest (~12h), (b) paid CDS pricing feed (Markit subscription, ~$X/mo), (c) prime-broker desk relationship to actually execute, and (d) live capital deployment. None of those exist yet. What does exist is the methodology — and the methodology is genuinely instrument-agnostic. The same BSI signal that fires on CVNA today would short CRVNA 2025-A junior tranches in v2; same data, cleaner expression.
