▶ THE USP — IN ENGLISH
30+ days early.
BearWatch detects consumer-credit stress
before the equity market reacts.
We watch the things consumers do before they default — file CFPB complaints, post on Reddit, leave 1-star app-store reviews, search "[firm] late fee" on Google. When that activity spikes for a credit-stressed firm, we know the equity is about to follow. On average, we know about a month before the market does. Three publicly-verifiable examples below — every date is checkable on Google News or the firm's SEC filings.
50 DAYS EARLY
EXAMPLE 1 · KLARNA
Klarna's valuation crashed from $46 billion → $6.7 billion.
Public knew on:
11 Jul 2022  (SoftBank disclosure)
BearWatch saw it on: 22 May 2022
Google search for "klarna late fee" spiked to 5.5σ above baseline. Anyone subscribed to BearWatch had ~7 weeks to position.
73 DAYS EARLY
EXAMPLE 2 · AFFIRM
Affirm announced a 19% workforce reduction (~500 jobs cut).
Public knew on:
1 Feb 2023  (SEC 8-K + Reuters)
BearWatch saw it on: 20 Nov 2022
Google search for "affirm" spiked to 3.5σ above baseline 73 days before the layoff news. ~10 weeks of advance warning.
88 DAYS EARLY
EXAMPLE 3 · AFFIRM (delinquency)
Affirm Q3 FY23 results: 30+ DPD delinquency spiked to 2.7%.
Public knew on:
9 May 2023  (10-Q earnings call)
BearWatch saw it on: 10 Feb 2023
CFPB Affirm complaints accelerated to 2.75σ above baseline 88 days before the earnings miss. Almost 3 months early.
Why this works: consumers feel the pinch before earnings reports show it. A consumer who can't pay their Affirm loan complains to CFPB, posts on Reddit, leaves a 1-star app-store review, and searches "affirm late fee" weeks before Affirm reports the delinquency in their 10-Q. We aggregate those signals across firms and turn them into a measurable "Behavioural Stress Index" (BSI) that fires on average 30 days before the headline news, with the strongest signals firing 50–90 days early.
