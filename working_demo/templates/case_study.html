The conditional case: Under the headline event-date convention (Mar 2024 Chapter 11 filing) used in the paper's sensitivity table, CURO is detected — first BSI alert fires 720 calendar days ahead at the 2σ threshold. Under a tighter event-date convention that uses CURO's first earnings warning rather than the eventual filing, the detection becomes a near-miss, because regulator-driven origination choke-off produced a quiet runoff (fewer originations → fewer customer interactions → fewer complaints) even as the surviving book deteriorated.

Post-mortem
Honest publishable claim: 5/5 firm-level (100%), conditional on event-date rule ▾
Headline sensitivity under the bankruptcy-filing convention = 5/5 firms detected = 100%, with Wilson 95% CI [56.6%, 100%]. Under a tighter event-date convention CURO becomes conditional. The paper §6.10 reports both.
disclosed limitation
What it means for trade construction: any strategy whose event-date rule diverges from "bankruptcy-filing" inherits the conditionality on quiet-runoff failure modes. Strategies that use BSI as a SCREEN (only trade when it fires, ignore everything else) will have higher precision but lower recall on quiet-runoff cases. The fixed-income instrument deployment (paper §9.1) adds CDS-spread divergence as a parallel signal to recover quiet-runoff cases that complaint-volume alone misses.
Lead with it — own the conditionality explicitly rather than hide it.

Trading at ~12–14× forward EBITDA throughout. This is not the profile of a reflexive small-float rally; this is fundamental expansion.

The methodology insight: the BSI numerator measures absolute complaint volume; at growth-stage issuers, that numerator scales mechanically with the active-customer denominator. Complaints per active customer at SEZL trended sideways or down across the window even as the absolute count rose 9×. The fix is a denominator normalisation: c̃ᵢₜ = Cᵢₜ / Nᵢₜ_cust using 10-Q active-customer disclosures (paper §7.3). The 4-gate architecture (BSI ∧ SCP ∧ MOVE ∧ CCD) and the 6-archetype War Room provide additional protection, but the structural fix is denominator normalisation.

What SEZL proves (and doesn't)

What SEZL proves
  • Pure z-threshold strategies will produce catastrophic false positives
  • The 4-gate AND-architecture is necessary, not optional
  • Equity is the wrong instrument when meme-momentum dominates the regime
  • Signal accuracy ≠ trade profitability when instrument is reflexive
  • This is the empirical justification for the entire v2.0 fixed-income pivot
What SEZL doesn't prove
  • The signal was wrong about consumer friction (it was right — absolute complaint volume did rise 9× and the consumer-side stress was real)
  • That SEZL was a meme-driven rally — fundamentals (66% YoY revenue, 60% EBIT margin, S&P SmallCap 600 inclusion) tell a different story
  • That every BSI alert is suspect — 5/5 firms in the universe were detected (Wilson 95% CI [56.6%, 100%]); SEZL is a denominator-normalisation gap, not a signal failure
